## From Keyboard to Playground 🏏

As a front-end developer, most of my day is spent sitting, typing and staring at a screen. So when I get free time, you'll usually find me outside with a bat, a ball or a paddle in my hand.

I'm a proper **sports freak**, and three games take up most of my heart: **cricket, volleyball and pickleball**.

## Cricket: My First Love 🏏

In India, cricket is more than a game; it's an emotion. For me, it started on gullies and open grounds with friends, a tennis ball and a makeshift wicket.

**What cricket gives me:**

- **Patience**: Building an innings takes time. You can't hit every ball.
- **Handling pressure**: Needing 10 runs in the last over is nerve-wracking and thrilling.
- **Team spirit**: One player doesn't win a match; the whole team does.
- **Resilience**: You get out, and you come back next match.

Honestly, debugging a tricky bug feels a lot like facing a tough bowler. You stay focused, read the pattern, and wait for the right ball.

## Volleyball: Power of Teamwork 🏐

Volleyball is fast, energetic and completely team-driven. You can't play it alone. Every point needs a receive, a set and a spike working together.

**Lessons from the court:**

- **Trust your teammates**: you pass knowing someone will set it up.
- **Communication**: calling the ball loudly avoids collisions and confusion.
- **Quick reactions**: there's no time to overthink.
- **Roles matter**: everyone contributes differently, and everyone is important.

This is exactly how a good development team works: designers, developers and testers passing the ball smoothly until we score a great release.

## Pickleball: The New Favourite 🥒

Pickleball is the newest addition to my routine, and I'm hooked. It mixes ideas from tennis, badminton and table tennis, it's easy to pick up, and it's a lot of fun.

**Why I love it:**

- **Beginner-friendly**: you can enjoy it from your very first game.
- **Social**: matches are short, friendly and full of laughter.
- **Great workout**: quick movement, reflexes and footwork without feeling like a chore.
- **All ages welcome**: I've seen people of very different ages enjoying the same court.

The "kitchen" (the non-volley zone) and the soft dinking game keep you thinking, which makes it more strategic than it looks.

## How Sports Help My Work

| On the field | At my desk |
|---|---|
| Staying calm under pressure | Handling deadlines and production issues |
| Teamwork and communication | Collaborating with designers and developers |
| Learning from mistakes | Fixing bugs without frustration |
| Discipline and practice | Consistent learning and improvement |
| Energy and fitness | Better focus and productivity |

## Tips for Developers Who Want to Start Playing

1. **Schedule it**: treat your game like a meeting you can't skip.
2. **Start with friends**: it's easier to stay consistent.
3. **Warm up and stretch**: long hours of sitting make injuries more likely.
4. **Hydrate well**, especially in warm weather.
5. **Play for fun**: winning is a bonus; showing up is the goal.

## Final Whistle

Sports remind me that life isn't only about screens and sprints. A sweaty game with friends recharges me like nothing else, and I go back to my code with a clearer mind and a bigger smile.

So, whether it's cricket, volleyball or pickleball: **close the laptop, grab your gear, and go play.** 🏆
