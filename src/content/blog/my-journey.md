## Hello, I'm Devang 👋

I'm **Devang Suthar**, a front-end developer working at an IT company. By day I turn designs into fast, accessible and good-looking interfaces. Outside work, I'm a sports lover who plays cricket, volleyball and pickleball, and a volunteer with the **Robin Hood Army**, where I serve food and share smiles with people in need on weekends.

This blog is my space to share what I learn, what I love, and what keeps me going. Let me start at the beginning.

## How It Started

Like many developers, my story began with curiosity. I was fascinated by how a web page appears on the screen: how a few lines of HTML can become a button, how CSS can make it feel alive, and how JavaScript makes it respond to a click.

My first pages were simple and honestly a bit ugly. But the feeling of seeing something I built show up in the browser was addictive. That feeling is what pushed me toward front-end development.

## Learning the Craft

The front-end world never stands still. Along the way I worked through:

- **HTML and CSS**: semantic markup, Flexbox, Grid, responsive design
- **JavaScript**: the language that makes the web interactive
- **Modern frameworks**: component-based thinking with tools like React
- **Performance and accessibility**: because a great UI should be fast and usable by everyone
- **Modern tooling**: Git, build tools, and frameworks like **Astro**, which is what powers this blog

The biggest lesson? **Consistency beats intensity.** An hour a day over months taught me more than a few weekend marathons ever did.

## Life as a Front-End Developer

Working in an IT company has taught me skills that no tutorial covers:

1. **Collaboration**: working with designers, back-end developers, testers and product teams.
2. **Communication**: explaining technical ideas in simple words.
3. **Attention to detail**: a 2px misalignment can matter more than you'd think.
4. **Adaptability**: requirements change, and that's okay.
5. **Ownership**: caring about the final experience, not just finishing tickets.

Some days are smooth. Other days I spend hours on one stubborn CSS bug that turns out to be a missing semicolon. Both kinds of days make me better.

## Finding Balance

Sitting in front of a screen all day has a cost. Two things keep me balanced:

- **Sports** clear my head, keep me fit, and teach me teamwork. (More in my post *Sports Freak*.)
- **Volunteering** keeps me grounded and reminds me why the work we do matters. (More in my post *Volunteering with Robin Hood Army*.)

Code makes me a better professional. Sports make me a healthier person. Service makes me a more grateful one.

## What I've Learned So Far

- Be a **lifelong learner**; no one knows everything.
- **Small projects** teach more than big plans that never start.
- **Health and kindness** are as important as career growth.
- **Share what you learn**; teaching others is the best way to learn.

## What's Next

I want to keep building better user experiences, go deeper into performance and accessibility, write more here, and keep showing up on the field and on the streets for the people who need a hot meal.

If you're a beginner who's just starting out, my advice is simple: **start small, stay curious, and don't compare your chapter 1 to someone else's chapter 20.**
